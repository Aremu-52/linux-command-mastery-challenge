**Phase 3 – Users, Groups & Package Management | Day 12 of 30**

## Commands covered today
See [commands.md](./commands.md) for all 10 commands with syntax and my own explanation of what each one does and when I would reach for it.

## What I practiced
I created a group named `devs`, added two users to it, confirmed membership with `getent`, removed one member, and finally deleted the group entirely.

## What surprised me
I was surprised how easy it is to manage group membership with `gpasswd` and how clearly `getent group` shows the members of a group.
