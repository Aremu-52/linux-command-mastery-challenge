# Day 21: Viewing Processes

**Phase 5 – Process & Service Management | Day 21 of 30**

## Commands covered today
See [commands.md](./commands.md) for all 10 commands with syntax and my own explanation of what each one does and when I would reach for it.

## What I practiced
I listed running processes with different `ps` options, monitored them live with `top` and `htop`, found PIDs with `pgrep`, viewed the process tree with `pstree`, checked open ports with `lsof`, and practiced adjusting process priority with `nice` and `renice`.

## What surprised me
I was surprised how much information `ps aux` and `pstree` give about what is running on the system, and how easy it is to find which process is using a specific port with `lsof`.
