## What I practiced
I provisioned a complete new team member account (created user, added to a group, set password) and installed the tools they needed (tree, curl, git). I also practiced checking installed and upgradable packages and cleaned up with autoremove.

## What surprised me
I was surprised how smoothly the whole process of creating a user and installing their required tools can be done in a short, clear sequence of commands.
