# Day 19: Vim Navigation & Search/Replace

**Phase 4 – Environment, Vim & Text Processing | Day 19 of 30**

## Commands covered today
See [commands.md](./commands.md) for all 10 commands with syntax and my own explanation of what each one does and when I would reach for it.

## What I practiced
I practiced jumping to the top and bottom of a file, going to a specific line, searching forward and backward, moving between matches, doing a global search and replace, deleting words and characters, opening new lines, and saving + quitting with ZZ.

## What surprised me
I was surprised how fast navigation becomes once you know `gg`, `G`, `:10`, `/`, and `n`. Combined with `:%s/old/new/g`, editing large files in Vim feels much more powerful.
