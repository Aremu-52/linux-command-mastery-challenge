## Commands covered today
See [commands.md](./commands.md) for all 10 commands with syntax and my own explanation of what each one does and when I would reach for it.

## What I practiced
I opened a file in Vim, entered Insert mode, returned to Command mode, saved the file, practiced saving and quitting, quitting without saving, deleting a line, copying and pasting a line, and using Undo/Redo.

## What surprised me
I was surprised how powerful the simple combination of `dd`, `yy`, `p`, `u`, and `Ctrl + r` is for quick editing once you get used to Command mode.
